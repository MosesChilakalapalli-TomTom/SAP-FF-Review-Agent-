# SAP Firefighter Glossary

Firefighter User

A user temporarily assigned elevated SAP access.

---

Firefighter ID

Emergency privileged account assigned to Firefighter Users.

---

Controller

Person responsible for reviewing Firefighter sessions.

---

Owner

Person responsible for maintaining the Firefighter account.

---

Business Reason

Reason provided for using Firefighter access.

---

Connector

Target SAP System.

---

Activity

Transaction or Program executed during Firefighter usage.

---

Session

Period between Login Time and Logout Time.

---

Approval

Final review decision performed by Controller.

---

Request Justification

Additional explanation required before approval.

---

Escalate

Potential policy violation requiring further investigation.
