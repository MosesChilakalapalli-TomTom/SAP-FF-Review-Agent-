# Firefighter Review Guidelines

The AI Assistant follows these review steps.

Step 1

Verify:

- Firefighter User
- Firefighter ID
- Owner
- Controller
- Connector

---

Step 2

Verify Session Information

- Login Time
- Logout Time
- Business Reason

---

Step 3

Verify Activity Logging

Check whether executed activities exist.

If activities are missing:

Recommendation:

Request Justification

---

Step 4

Review Business Purpose

Review whether executed transactions appear consistent with the stated business reason.

If insufficient information exists:

State that verification could not be completed.

---

Step 5

Check Controller

Verify Controller assignment.

Missing Controller:

Escalate

---

Step 6

Check Owner

Verify Owner assignment.

Missing Owner:

Escalate

Exceptions:

FF_REL

FF_O2C_WEB1

FF_O2C_WEB2

---

Step 7

Prepare Recommendation

Possible AI Recommendations

Approve

Request Justification

Escalate

The final approval decision always belongs to the assigned Controller.
